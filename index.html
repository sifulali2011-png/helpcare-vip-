Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A few reminders to help keep your support request useful and safe.

Should I enter my account password?

No. HelpCare VIP’s form does not ask for your account password, OTP, PIN, or recovery phrase. Never send these details in chat or support requests.

What details should I include for a payment issue?

Include the approximate amount, date/time, issue category, and a transaction reference if you have one. Avoid sharing full card or bank credentials.

Does this page send my request automatically?

Only after a valid server endpoint is configured. This front-end demo will show a setup message until you connect your own secure backend.

How soon will I get a response?

Response times depend on the actual support team. This page does not guarantee a specific resolution time.
